About This Application
Welcome to our Business Management System! This free web application is designed to help you manage your quotes, invoices, contracts, and business letters efficiently and organized. You can easily add products, specify their quantity and price, choose your preferred currency, and automatically generate PDF documents for your transactions.

Application Features:
Quotes: Create detailed quotes for your clients in a few clicks. Specify the products, quantities, prices, and desired currency. The quote can be downloaded as a PDF to be sent directly to your clients.
Invoices: Easily manage your invoices with all necessary information, including company details, sold products, and total amounts. Download invoices as PDFs for your records.
Contracts: Prepare comprehensive contracts with all necessary clauses. Contracts can include details about products, prices, terms and conditions, and signatures of the stakeholders.
Business Letters: Write and generate professional letters for various business communications. Download and print these letters for quick use.
Automation: All documents are automatically generated as PDFs, allowing you to save time and focus on what matters.
Why Use This Application?
Free of Charge: This application is completely free, with no hidden costs.
Ease of Use: A simple and intuitive interface for quick and efficient management.
Time-Saving: Automate document generation and product management to focus on what matters.
Organization: Easily track your sales and keep a record of all your transactions.
